Privacy

The Internet Mansion Project VIP list. In plain English.

What we collect

Your Instagram handle, your phone number or email, your city, your room, your age group, your roster choice, and your picture if you add one.

What we use it for

To put you on the disco ball, give you a room, run the party invite list, and text or email you about the mansion and the party.

What people can see

Your handle and approved picture can appear on the disco ball, in our videos, and on our site. If you opt in, your handle and room appear on your room's roster. Your phone number, email, city, and age stay private.

Who else gets it

We don't sell your information. We store it with Google, and we may use a texting or email service to send messages.

Texts and emails

Reply STOP to any text or use the unsubscribe link in any email to stop getting them. Message and data rates may apply.

Age

You must be at least 13 to sign up. The party is 18+.

Delete your info

DM @internetmansionproject and ask. We'll remove your signup and picture.
